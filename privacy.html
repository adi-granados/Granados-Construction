Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Granados General Contractor ("we", "us") operates granadoscontractor.com. This page explains what information the website handles and what we do with it.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the contact form: your name, email address, and optionally your phone number, project location, and project details. This information is sent to us by email so we can respond to your inquiry. It is not stored in a database on this website.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking tools of any kind.

How we use your information

We use the information you send only to respond to your inquiry and to provide estimates and services you request. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone for marketing purposes.

Service providers

The website is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operation. Contact form messages are delivered by email through Resend. These providers handle data only to provide their services to us.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any messages you have sent us, or ask what information we have from you, by contacting us through the form on this website.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
