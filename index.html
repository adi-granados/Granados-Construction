What We Do

Services

Framing is our specialty and the foundation of our business. We also carry projects through the rest of the exterior envelope, so you can work with one contractor from start to finish.

Our Specialty

Framing

New construction and remodel framing done to plan and to code. Walls, floor systems, roof framing, trusses and rafters, laid out accurately so every trade after us has a clean, true structure to work from.

  • New home and garage framing
  • Floor systems, walls and roof structures
  • Remodel and structural framing

Window Installation

New and replacement windows installed square, flashed and sealed properly to keep water and drafts out.

Deck Framing

Solid deck substructures with correct footings, ledger attachment, beams and joists, ready for the decking of your choice.

Additions

Room additions, second stories and garages framed and tied into your existing home cleanly and securely.

Roofing

Roof replacements and new roofs installed with attention to underlayment, flashing and ventilation.

Siding Installation

Siding installed over a properly prepared wall with clean lines, tight trim and careful detailing.

About Us

A framing company first. A general contractor you can rely on.

Granados General Contractor is a Southwest Michigan construction company built around one core skill: framing. The frame is the skeleton of every building. When it is out of square or out of plumb, every trade after it pays the price. We take that responsibility seriously.

From that foundation we have grown to handle window installation, deck framing, additions, roofing and siding, so homeowners and builders can keep more of their project with one trusted crew.
